## The Scenario

You're analyzing bee diversity data from urban gardens. Your dataset includes a column called "Garden Type" with these entries:

-   Community garden
-   Community Garden (capital G)
-   community garden (lowercase)\
-   Comm. garden
-   School garden
-   school garden (community-maintained)
-   Private yard
-   private yard (open to public)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Your Task

Write a decision log entry explaining how you would standardize these categories for analysis — and why.

Use the structure below. **Take the full 10 minutes** to think through your reasoning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Decision Log Entry

### Decision:

*What did you decide to do? Be specific about how you'll standardize these categories.*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Reasoning:

*Why did you make this decision? What makes it defensible?*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Alternative(s) Considered:

*What other approaches did you think about? Why didn't you choose them?*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Trade-off(s):

*What are you gaining and losing with this decision? What's the cost of your choice?*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Reflection (optional - if you have extra time)

What was hardest about writing this?

What surprised you about the process?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

*Remember: There's no single "right" answer. The goal is clear, defensible reasoning.*
